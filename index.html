Reiki is a gentle Japanese energy healing practice that supports your body’s natural ability to restore balance and well-being.

Developed by Dr. Mikao Usui in the early 20th century, Reiki is now practiced worldwide as a complementary approach to emotional, physical, and spiritual harmony.

During a session, the practitioner places their hands lightly on or just above the body, allowing energy to flow where it is most needed. Many people experience deep relaxation, emotional release, and a renewed sense of clarity and peace.
